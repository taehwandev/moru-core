# Marketing Planning

Use for an audience, positioning, message, channel or campaign brief before
content production or live optimization. Existing funnel telemetry, traffic
allocation and CRO implementation remain owned by
`product-patterns/skills/growth-conversion-optimization/SKILL.md`.

Read `references/current-guidance.md`. For a brief consumed by later work, also
read `common/skills/product-spec-to-implementation/references/artifact-handoff.md`.

## Process

1. Inspect product evidence, audience knowledge and the requested outcome.
2. Draft audience/message hypotheses and one bounded learning experiment.
3. Hand the brief to writing/design or the existing CRO owner as appropriate.

## Stop If

A requested claim or live action lacks evidence or matching authority. Continue
the safe draft without inventing performance, customers or an approved budget.

## Verification And Report

Check factual support, audience fit, the primary outcome and its denominator,
guardrails, stopping rule and unresolved decisions. Report drafts separately
from published campaigns or measured results.
