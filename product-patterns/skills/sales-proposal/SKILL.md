# Sales Discovery And Proposal

Use to structure a prospect's needs, assess fit or prepare a tailored sales
proposal/discovery brief. Product requirements, campaign planning and legal
contract review remain with their existing owners.

Read `references/current-guidance.md`. For a proposal used by later work, also
read `common/skills/product-spec-to-implementation/references/artifact-handoff.md`.

## Process

1. Inspect customer needs and seller-approved capabilities and commercial facts.
2. Separate known qualification evidence, assumptions and unanswered questions.
3. Draft a bounded proposal and explicitly unresolved terms without commitments.

## Stop If

An unsupported claim or unapproved commercial commitment would be necessary.
Return the useful internal draft and name the missing decision or evidence.

## Verification And Report

Check need-to-capability fit, evidence, scope, commercial terms and authority.
Report a draft separately from a sent offer, CRM update or accepted deal.
